Humanity did not leave Earth out of ambition. In 2150 scientists confirmed the planet's magnetosphere was failing, and while trying to work out why, they made it worse: gravity-warping experiments meant to unlock deep-space travel instead stripped away what atmosphere Earth had left. Earth itself was fully evacuated by 2203 and now sits in the Sol system as a cold, irradiated reminder of the trade humanity made.

What rebuilt itself out among the stars split cleanly in two: the United Colonies, bureaucratic and well-armed, and the Freestar Collective, the frontier's looser answer to it. Everyone else — explorers, pirates, zealots, one deeply enthusiastic drinks-and-drugs megacorporation — operates in the gaps between the two. Use Browse the Guide above to look any of it up.
